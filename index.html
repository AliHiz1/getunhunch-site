FAQ

Questions

Do I need AirPods, a watch or a wearable?

No. Unhunch uses your phone’s own motion sensor and scheduled reminders. Nothing else to buy or wear.

How does it know I’ve been sitting?

Your phone notices when it hasn’t moved with you for a while. When you walk around, the timer resets. You can also tap “I got up” yourself.

Will it spam me with notifications?

Reminders only come during the work hours you set, at the interval you choose. Weekends are off by default.

What happens to my data?

Your activity and stats stay on your phone. No account is needed to use Unhunch.

Is this medical advice?

No. Unhunch is a general wellness app, not a medical device or treatment. If something hurts, stop and talk to a professional.
